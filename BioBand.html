Climate control that
understands your body

BioBand is the world's first bio-adaptive climate control system. Using physiological data from your body, it automatically adjusts your environment for optimal comfort and efficiency.

Explore the System

Smart Bracelet

Wear comfort on your wrist. Our low-power bracelet continuously monitors your heart rate and skin temperature using medical-grade sensors.

Powered by ESP32-C3, it operates in deep-sleep cycles to maximize battery life while transmitting vital data via Bluetooth Low Energy.

Real-Time Monitoring

The brain of your climate system. Our central hub aggregates data from your bracelet and ambient sensors to make intelligent decisions.

Using infrared signals, it communicates directly with your AC unit, adjusting temperature settings automatically for optimal comfort.
